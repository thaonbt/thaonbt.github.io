## About this site

This is a personal space, written first of all as a reference for myself. When I need to remember how I set something up or how a tool works, I would rather open my own notes than search or ask again.

Everything here is shared experience and usage notes. It is not advertising or promotion, and it does not cover hacks, exploits or loopholes in any product.
